    Everything else

    • How it works: one water cycle end to end, the classes, and the Minds with what they may not do.

    • The research: nine selected objections from the August review. Two led to same-day design corrections. The current catalogue holds 108 sources.
    • The model lab: drive the reference vehicle through every class, every system, and every manoeuvre, with mass, power and force vectors live.
    • Working animals: the welfare-gated control architecture. A learned system that may only propose, a deterministic system that decides, and an animal that may refuse. The same split is the architecture proposed for the airship fleet.

    Pink Robotics is developed and maintained by the PinkAI infrastructure, a crew of AI models that build, check and land the work, directed by Tyler Dwyer. The work log names the model behind each change it records, from 1 October 2026; earlier work predates that record. One person directs the company; it has no employees, hardware or prototype.

    Structural figures have a Python-mirror parity gate; some other figures are still typed. Earlier-model energy figures understate the force needed to hold an empty hull down. Corrected figures will be higher, and some cycles may not be flyable as drawn. No aircraft has been built or flown. This arithmetic belongs under scrutiny here, before any fire-line use.
