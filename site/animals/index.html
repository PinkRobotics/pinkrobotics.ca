The animal partnership

The animal would be a partner.

The proposal pairs a persistent AI with one working animal, for that animal's life. Sensors and longitudinal memory would support the animal's mobility and judgement; comparative performance has not been measured. The system may only propose; it does not control. The design preserves the animal's agency and right to refuse from the first line. It uses the Five Domains welfare model, and the commitments appear below.

The proposed control split also appears in the airship fleet. Here is what it aims to protect. The airships carry the company's focus, and the twelve-article covenant is versioned in the repository, unchanged.

How control is split

Also proposed for the airship fleet

The generative model may only propose an animal-facing action, as a typed request carrying purpose, expected benefit, urgency, confidence and operating-domain state. A deterministic welfare system outside the model decides, and may refuse. The animal may decline, and a declined cue never triggers automatic escalation. The same split is proposed for the airship fleet's architecture. It is a design in both places: no controller has been built.

The welfare covenant

Twelve articles, versioned

Welfare is engineered in, on the Five Domains model and animal-computer-interaction research. Twelve articles, kept in the repository. Eight of them:

1
Stakeholder statusThe animal is a beneficiary with standing, not an asset, a sensor platform or an actuator. The operation's own productivity is not enough to call a deployment successful.
2
Net welfare benefitEvery deployment has to identify the welfare benefit to the animal itself, not only to the operation around it.
3
Non-aversive by enumerationNo electric shock, no pain, no fear, no deprivation of food, water or company, no exhaustion, no escalating intimidation. Defined by list, not by tone.
4
Refusal is informationA declined cue is examined for hazard, confusion, fatigue, fear, pain or competing motivation before anything else. The system never answers a refusal with more intensity.
5
Assent, not complianceA trained animal is not counted as consenting because it complies. Assent is read from repeated observable choices: voluntary approach, willingness to re-engage.
6
Rest and privacyTime in which the animal is not instrumented, not cued, and not observed.
7
An ordered conflict ruleWhen interests collide: immediate safety of people and animals first, then prevention of serious suffering, and only then the payer's interest.
8
An independent councilVeterinarians for participating species, a welfare scientist, an animal-computer-interaction researcher, a positive-reinforcement trainer and outside voices would sit before any live trial, with power to pause it.

Twelve articles are drafted in full for a council seated before any animal wears a device. The first partners are planned to be dogs and cattle: animals that already work alongside people, with jobs, handlers, and sensor hardware that already exists for them.

The roadmap

Five phases, held in order by welfare gates

Five phases over six years, held in order by welfare gates rather than dates. Phase zero is bench work on instrumented body surrogates and mechanical rigs; no animal is involved until phase one, and only after the council approves. Instrumentation precedes cooperation. Phase one is brief wear trials with no autonomous cues. Autonomy arrives four to six years in, gated by welfare, not by dates.

What comes first

In order, welfare-gated

First the council is seated. Then phase-zero bench work runs on instrumented surrogates and mechanical rigs, and no animal is involved until phase one. The covenant's twelve articles are versioned in the repository.

What is not claimed

  • Designed, not deployed: no animal has worn a device, and none will until the council is seated.
  • Nothing here establishes clinical validity or regulatory standing. No agricultural or veterinary authority has reviewed the project.
  • The covenant guides the design today and would bind operations if they begin. The council must be seated before any animal wears a device. Article 8 makes that a condition of any trial.
