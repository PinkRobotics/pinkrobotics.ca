The two questions

Does it stand? Every named structural check, margin by margin, at the declared safety factor. That includes the operator's requirement that the wall stand as a structure before it is ever pumped down. Does it float? The whole component ledger against the air the hull displaces, at both altitudes, across every world the record cannot yet choose between.

As drawn, the 52 m hull does not float. The record basis uses structural safety factor 1.2 against full sea-level pressure, knockdown 0.30 and 1,050 MPa chords. Its lift is 0.558 of its mass at sea level and 0.436 at 2,500 m. The favourable basis assumes knockdown 0.65 and a 1,450 MPa carbon-laminate compressive ceiling, both unverified, at the same safety factor. Its lift-to-mass ratios are 0.981 at sea level and 0.766 at 2,500 m: short by 4.3 t and 53.6 t respectively.

Closing the gap needs knockdown tests, chord coupons and a complete mass bill. The chordal net and in-surface shear credits are bounds, with geometry or hardware still unpriced; neither establishes a drawn floating hull.

The bill and the drawing disagree in the end caps, in both directions. Across five readings the favourable lift-to-mass ratio spans 0.751–0.998 at sea level and 0.586–0.780 at 2,500 m. No reading reaches 1; none is a checked design.

The drawing and the bill disagree in 20 places, recorded for the float ledger in the member census.

The two review rounds

A second gpt-6 run and glm-5.3 reproduced the census's three corrections; muse-spark-1.3 examined the float case, which records both. No person checked the arithmetic.

This page's first draft said the ship floats (+6.4 t at the declared factor). Three adversarial reviewers were set on the physics before any page quoted it, and they confirmed eleven bugs. Among them: a missing divisor in the general-instability law, a shear stiffness built on the wrong leg of a triangle, a brace pitch the drawn fan cannot produce, and caps that escaped two of their duties. Fixed, the ship sank on the record basis but still floated on the favourable basis. A second panel rejected that remaining float verdict: a diametral spoke has no first-order stiffness against the odd out-of-round modes, exactly the mode that governed. The membrane term needs an in-surface shear path this wall does not have. Both fixes are in; the comparison above states the current result at both altitudes. Three things survived the whole fight the honest way:

  • The ring-plane X-webs are a member class no drawing had, without which the two walls cannot act as one deep ring. They are now drawn, priced, and gated.
  • The spokes — the fallback the record had already licensed: pretensioned cords across the void, a Winkler foundation against the EVEN out-of-round modes. t of cord in the ledger today; the chordal layout that would also work the odd modes is SHIP-3's named design move.
  • The verdict gates: a positive sea-level margin is reported only when the model's float condition passes. That condition does not establish a buildable hull.

The two walls: crush and sink

The crush wall is the model's greedy sizing at safety factor 1.0 against full sea-level pressure. It is not a proven minimum, and some checks retain excess capacity. The sink wall is displaced-air lift. Their difference is a model mass band, not proof of a buildable design. The neutral ceiling compares fixed mass with air density; it does not resize the structure or verify a hull.

SF_float is the largest factor this sizing search places below the sea-level sink wall; a closed band has no such factor. This is an arithmetic bound, not a floating design. At the 2,500 m working altitude the sink wall drops to t and every world's band as drawn is negative — the altitude ladder is a design input, not a detail. What would have to be true

  • The GI knockdown lands near frame practice. SHIP-2's test campaign, the single biggest lever on the ship, worth roughly two hundred tonnes of verdict.
  • SHIP-3's two named design moves land: the chordal spoke net (odd modes at fibre weight) and an in-surface shear system (licenses the membrane term the honest model currently zeroes). If adopted, both credits would remain bounds: the chordal geometry is undrawn and the shear hardware is unpriced. Neither establishes a floating hull at the working altitude.
  • The chord coupons land above the working estimate. The second lever; decides the world inside whichever knockdown holds.
  • The spokes hold their premise: pretension without creep-walk (Dyneema-class cord at near-zero mean load, or a stiffer fibre), terminations at -scale part counts.
  • The drape licenses the two-way film credit. The model prices the film one-way until it does.
  • η holds at % at ship joint counts, and the Ti fitting masses price at their estimates.

Every flag above is carried in the model itself and travels with its number onto every page that binds it. The definition of done is unchanged from FLOAT.md: a gated bill of materials under the wall with the declared factor on every failure mode, and nothing dropped to get there.
