The band: choose a hull between the two walls

The crush wall is the model's greedy sizing at safety factor 1.0 against full sea-level pressure. It is not a proven minimum, and some checks retain excess capacity. The sink wall is displaced-air lift at the selected altitude. Their difference is a model mass band, not proof of a buildable design. Drag the sliders to compare fixed structural masses with altitude-dependent lift. The calculations come from ship/model.js, held to the Python record by the parity gate.
